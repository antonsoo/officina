# Two ways to read a slope

Seked adds a source-led reading of Egyptian geometry. Gonia's new entasis
workshop becomes its catalogue destination while the spacing and corner
workshops remain reachable. The catalogue contains 23 projects, including
nine in Ancient world.

| Project | Destination | Concrete reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seked | Rhind problem 59, stated dimensions, step 4, half and quarter selected | Switch from the stated dimensions to those used in the working: the calculated seked changes from 7/3 to 21/4 palms. The literal edition's final 11/2 remains a separate result. |
| Gonia | Parthenon flute-edge survey, first interior station, circle comparison | Compare the circle's prediction with a measured point not used to fit it; inspect the middle row where the printed hyperbola table differs from its prose. |

Both destinations have complete source pages, portable illustrated readings,
and independently verified offline editions. The catalogue's evidence links
pin reviewed source commits rather than moving branches. Their descriptions
retain the older search terms: “Doric”, “Vitruvius” and “column spacing”.

The new thumbnails are actual public-site screenshots at 1200 by 750 pixels.
They contain original diagrams and prose, with notices in
`public/thumbs/ATTRIBUTION.txt`. The capture receipt records their exact public
URLs and image hashes.

`scripts/ancient-journeys.mjs` exercises catalogue search and keyboard activation,
then uses each lesson's native controls. The Seked journey reconstructs the
half-plus-quarter operation and opens Peet's correction. The Gonia journey
opens the table and follows navigation to both older workshops. Each then
returns to the catalogue and opens the immutable GitHub evidence link. Its
heading must land visibly through native fragment navigation.

The earlier [four-reading record](ancient-readings-2026-10-21.md) remains an
account of its own published revision. Current publication and browser evidence
is retained in [the geometry-reading receipts](evidence/geometry-readings-2026-10-21/).
