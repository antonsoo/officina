learning projects. Haidao adds a focused Chinese geometry lesson: the received
commentary's pole-gap divisor passes one sighting but fails the other. Its
smaller editorial correction uses the four-bu retreat difference instead.

The card opens an applied source record at operation zero, with the received
divisor chosen and the correction's Chinese passage selected. The learner
performs the six operations, checks the second line, changes divisors, reads the
whole opening and tries a modern setback interval. The card's evidence link
targets [the reviewed source revision](https://github.com/antonsoo/haidao/blob/f42a7c4681799710aa617a7bcc14e29a21335580/docs/review.md#try-the-received-wording).

`−4980/127 bu`; independent ±2-bu setback intervals give no finite upper height
bound. This is the app's modern exact model of an idealized source exercise,
not evidence for an actual expedition or historical measurement precision.
