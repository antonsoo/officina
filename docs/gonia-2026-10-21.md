# Gonia discovery, 2026-10-21

Gonia joins the Ancient world catalogue. The demo opens a beam depth of 2T
with a widened end metope: a 2T panel beside ordinary 1.5T panels. A learner can
contract the end bay instead and see its spacing change from 5T to 4.5T while
the corner triglyph remains 0.5T off the column axis.

The recorded-evidence link pins the coordinate constraints and source limits
at Gonia revision `4ffadaced8ea90b41a826daa451480083e8128d0`. The thumbnail is an
actual workshop screenshot taken at the catalogue aspect ratio; it keeps the
outer corner visible.

`node scripts/gonia-journey.mjs BASE_URL OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` checks search for
“Doric”, the loaded thumbnail, native keyboard navigation to the public demo,
the actual changed measurements, and the visible pinned heading on GitHub.
Chromium and Firefox are exercised at 1280 and 375 pixels. The initial local
catalogue review also passed 38 unit checks and 32 browser scenarios.

The evidence directory records local journeys, public journeys, screenshots
and canonical hosted-byte verification separately. Those receipts establish
navigation and publication, not a new historical validation of Gonia.
