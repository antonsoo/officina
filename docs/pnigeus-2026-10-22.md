# Finding the water-organ workshop

The catalogue adds Pnigeus to Ancient world. Its live link opens three pump
strokes followed by one pipe releasing for five seconds at tank area ratio 6.
The resulting pressure is about 410 Pa. The discovery text keeps this result
inside its explicitly chosen teaching model.

The evidence destination pins Pnigeus's public source and publication review.
It records the independent gas calculation, rendered water levels, actual
measurement and sound downloads, and hosted/offline browser workflows.

The thumbnail is an actual 1200 by 750 capture of the public experiment,
showing both water levels and the 410 Pa reading. [Capture details](evidence/pnigeus-2026-10-22/thumbnails.json).

The [local catalogue journeys](evidence/pnigeus-2026-10-22/local-journeys.json)
use Chromium and Firefox at 1280 and 375 pixels. Each searches for “water organ”,
loads the thumbnail, follows the live link by keyboard, pumps and undoes,
reads the Latin note on water, decodes the source image, and follows the pinned
GitHub evidence link to its intended heading using native fragment navigation.
Public catalogue publication is recorded separately after deployment.
