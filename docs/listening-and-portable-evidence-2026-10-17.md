## A useful reference to hear

ToneMirror now includes Wolfdog's attributed 2019 Mandarin recording under
CC BY-SA 4.0. It is available before the first offline selection. Four short
excerpts and the complete sequence replace the need to invent a reference
before trying the tool. Recording credit survives selected-region WAV export,
renaming, reimport and saved-clip restoration. Damaged recognized credit is
rejected before replacing a usable clip.

Paired playback schedules both selected passages on the native audio clock,
plays each once at normal speed and stops the future attempt on cancellation.
The contour comparison remains visible while listening. Neither the source
PCM, individual half-speed setting nor WAV export changes. Native digital
renders cover 8, 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz. Firefox's 24 unavailable offline-suspend
controls stay explicitly unavailable; live-clock cancellation is checked in
both engines.

This is one speaker's isolated examples. The illustrated rising-versus-falling
comparison uses two excerpts from that recording, not a learner performance.
The tracker still makes documented pitch/octave errors, including classifying
the first tone as a low-confidence rise. The contour score is not a
pronunciation grade. No pitch detector or scoring threshold changed.

## A chart that survives leaving its report

Trainspotter's existing inline curves depended on their HTML page. Exporting
one now produces an independent, script-free SVG with a visible source/axis
caption and versioned JSON metadata. An incomplete input stays incomplete; an
excluded ambiguous metric does not gain a trajectory. A logging counter stays
**logging steps**. All 11 existing inline chart hashes across seven selected
cases remain identical; readers and detectors are unchanged.

Actual files are downloaded and reopened offline in both browser engines at
1440 and 375 pixels. A separate CairoSVG conversion exposed browser-missed
blank output, unresolved colors and clipped colored labels. Explicit viewport
dimensions, literal owned palette values and text positioning corrected these
before publication. The PDF conversion retains selectable text and contains
no raster images. These are digital checks, not measurements of a physical
printer or every SVG editor.

The script-free data link duplicates vector markup. The 200,000-observation
control grows from 99,272 to 218,040 HTML bytes, while retaining bounded display
geometry and its narrow spike. This is an explicit feature cost, not a size
optimization or new detector-calibration result. The catalogue keeps the
separately qualified historical calibration study.

## Repairs with observable consequences

TraceLens previously priced a constructed native span as a complete zero
charge despite its simultaneous declarations of zero and one million input
tokens. It now preserves both attributes, marks the quantity unknown and
withholds the charge. Equal declarations, including real zero, still count
once. Disagreement in an unused reasoning breakdown can retain a supported
inclusive-operand estimate with a stated assumption. User-supplied prices and
fictional model names keep this experiment separate from a provider invoice.
The 62 controlled cases go from 31 to 62 expected outcomes; all 342 retained
JSON/JSONL inputs keep their normalization and rejection results.

Veil's native-writer replay goes from 12 to 120 complete masks among 120
valid controlled documents; 12 captured SDK request bodies go from exposed
to whole-value masking. A separate syntax matrix goes from 144 to 2,160
complete masks, with exact restoration. All 1,483 retained public text files
keep their previous detection ledgers. This bounded recognizer is not a
complete YAML parser or a guarantee of arbitrary-secret detection.
